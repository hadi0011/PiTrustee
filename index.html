🌟 About PiTrustee

PiTrustee is the first decentralized trust and reputation system built for the Pi Network ecosystem. It enables users and service providers to build trust through transparent, community-driven verification.

PiTrustee is designed as a DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization), where the community — not a central authority — governs trust. Every vote carries weight based on the voter's engagement level, ensuring fair and reliable ratings.

Versionv1.0.0-BETA
Release Date2026
LicenseDecentralized & Community-owned
StatusBETA (Active Development)

🛠️ How It Works

  1. Providers Register: Service providers pay a one-time 1 Pi fee to register and submit their information for admin approval.
  2. Users Vote: Every KYC-verified Pi user can rate and review any provider with 1 to 5 stars. Votes are weighted by the user's Tier level.
  3. Community Trust Score: Each provider's final score is calculated using a weighted formula across all 4 tiers.
  4. Transparent Records: All votes and reviews are recorded on the Pi blockchain for full auditability.
  5. DAO Governance: Future decisions (fee adjustments, rule changes) will be voted by the community.

⭐ Rating System (4 Tiers)

Your vote weight depends on your activity in the Pi Network:

Tier 1 (KYC only)Weight: 1
Tier 2 (5+ transactions)Weight: 3
Tier 3 (20+ transactions & 1000+ Pi)Weight: 10
Tier 4 (Community Validator)Weight: 50

Final Score Formula: (Tier1 avg × 1) + (Tier2 avg × 3) + (Tier3 avg × 10) + (Tier4 avg × 50)
